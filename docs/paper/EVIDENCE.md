## Provenance of the results

The numbers are the Phase 5 results (commit `74ed751`, full dataset, 20 checkpoints trained on a Colab A100).
After that commit, `src/dataset.py` was changed to `.resample("2min").asfreq()`, which kept only rows whose
timestamp seconds were exactly `:00` (11,934 of 649,999 rows) and the checkpoints were retrained on that
subset. The full-data pipeline, the matching model code and the full-data checkpoints (hash-verified against the
`74ed751` manifest) were restored on `main`; the earlier state is on branch `checkpoint-2`.
